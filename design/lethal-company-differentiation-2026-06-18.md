# Lethal Company Differentiation Review — Synthesis (2026-06-18)

**Source:** 4-specialist parallel review (creative-director, game-designer, level-designer,
narrative-director), each grounded in the actual design docs. Triggered by PM question
"is this too similar to Lethal Company?"
**Status:** Direction memo (not a GDD). Captures the convergent findings + the action list.

## Verdict — MEDIUM (one line)

The extraction-loop **skeleton** is LC-shaped (inherent to the genre); Black Commission's identity is
genuinely its own, but it lives **downstream** (settlement satire, civic fiction, infection economics)
and is **mostly not yet in the runtime**. The current *build* plays more like LC than the *design* is.
The "not-LC" identity is carried roughly **80% by fiction, 20% by mechanics** — the inverse of what is
currently playable.

## ⭐ Unanimous finding — build the per-item settlement reveal

All four specialists, independently, named the **per-item settlement reveal** (each item's price + the
client's authored "intended use" note, shown in authored emotional order, with the one-dispute step) as
THE differentiator and the single highest-priority build. It is flagged Core/blocking
(`scavenging-core-loop.md` §3.7, Q4) but the current build shows a total-only card and the client notes
are unauthored.

> Ship total-only → reviewed as "Lethal Company with a civic skin." Ship the full reveal → not.

## Protect (genuine moat — non-LC)

- **Two-track economic squeeze** (License Stage + Earth Deterioration advancing independently of player
  choice); the Stay-on-Earth ending written in arithmetic.
- **Infection Exposure as a personal economic cost** (per-player 去污处理费 on the shared ledger) +
  item-condition degradation linking your body to your haul.
- **Echo Mold** — the team's own voice-chat is the attack surface (game-designer: "most irreplaceable system").
- **Per-item settlement satire** + the moral slope (Free Salvage → Commissioned → Black) visible on the ledger.
- **Dispatch-van ritual + outdoor biome approach** (3-zone van/outdoor/indoor vs LC's 2-zone); civic
  paperwork as a wayfinding language; vertical two-floor architecture; the power-gate as an irreversible
  mid-run commitment.

## Too close → concrete fix

- **Core loop shape** — genre-inherent; carry the difference downstream, don't fight the skeleton.
- **Weight-as-friction** (verbatim LC) → make "heavy" economically meaningful via condition + client
  category preference + the settlement reveal, not just physical encumbrance.
- **Danger = "building gets angrier over time"** (reads as a hidden timer) → frame as BC's own (dispatch
  office calling back with safety updates); make the multi-trip escalation legible.
- **Echo Mold counterplay risks collapsing to "stay silent"** = LC-style behavioural meta → make the
  replay *wrong in content* (a room that doesn't exist this seed, voice too close, a metallic harmonic) so
  counterplay is *recognition*, not silence. Keep silence costly (exposure accrues anyway).
- **First 60 seconds is the most LC-like surface** (van → dark building → flashlight → search) → put the
  paperwork at the **entry/approach**: commission form on the door, a prior team's route markers, the first
  interactable is a BC-7 form, not a random item.
- Stop defining the game reactively ("like LC but…") — describe the hero-room/biome system by what it *is*.

## ⚠️ Open tension vs a just-locked decision (C2)

creative-director recommends ONE *environmental* danger accelerator (e.g. restoring the power gate spikes
danger) to break the "hidden timer" feel and make the site respond to player action. This **conflicts with
the C2 decision locked 2026-06-18 ("danger = pure time, no action spikes").** Trade: action-triggered
escalation feels less like an LC countdown, but costs the predictability/balance of pure time.
**PM call — left open.**

## Prioritized actions (cross-agent)

1. **Build the per-item settlement reveal** (code: line-item expansion; content: authored client usage
   notes for the tower's item pool; UI: the approved `design/ux/settlement.md`). The only unanimous "now."
2. Author the **tower commission text** + **~5 dispute responses** in the institutional
   "条款 C-7 / 自动估价系统" register — establishes the brand voice; pure writing, no new system.
3. Put **paperwork at the entry**; render the **infection zone as a visual wayfinding layer** (mold
   thickening with depth = the danger gradient read without UI).
4. Author the safety-cost line item as a **named civic authority** (clause number), not a bare number.

## North-star (first 60 seconds — creative-director)

"I step out of the van and before I even enter, there is already paperwork — a commission form on the
door, a route arrow from a team that didn't come back — and I understand, with a specific dread, that I am
the *third* team to sign this exact job." That feeling is unreachable in Lethal Company.
